# Employee attendance privacy notice

> Draft operational privacy policy — requires review and approval by qualified legal/privacy counsel before production use in the applicable jurisdiction.

Version: `2026-09-08.draft-1`. Proposed notice; no attendance service or approved employee collection exists in the inspected implementation.

Before use, the employer must publish its legal identity, sites, monitored employee/privacy contact, specific attendance purpose, applicable legal ground, recipients, categories and retention schedule. The [separate biometric notice](biometric-and-face-recognition-notice.md) must be reviewed independently; employment or acceptance of this notice is not biometric consent.

## Proposed employee-facing explanation

“[Employer] would use [employee identifier and defined attendance event fields] at [site] to [specific attendance purpose]. Authorized [roles] would review those records. [SyncCam entity and named approved processors] would process them under [customer instructions and location]. Data would be retained for [approved period, trigger and legal exceptions].

“You can view and dispute your attendance through [working method]. A designated person checks corrections and provides a reasoned response through [channel]. Face/liveness results, if separately approved, may be inaccurate and will not automatically determine pay, discipline, dismissal or law-enforcement action. Ordinary payroll and employment recordkeeping obligations are assessed separately by the employer.

“[Badge/PIN/manual method] is available through [instructions]. Declining or withdrawing optional face processing does not eliminate this alternative. Withdrawal stops future consent-based biometric use and begins reviewed deletion; it does not necessarily erase records the employer must retain by law. We explain any exception and its duration through [contact].”

## Operational prerequisites

Review employment power imbalance, collective agreements, state/provincial employee distinctions, accessibility, minors and agency workers. Approve a necessity/proportionality assessment and practical alternative, including how missed or disputed clock events are corrected. Define who can see identifiable attendance and what the employer can export. Do not reuse security-camera events as an attendance database or treat generic `attendance_review` events as enrollment authority.

Implement subject verification, tenant/site authorization, purpose-specific consent where required, withdrawal propagation, review ownership, audit references and bounded correction reasons. Complete the [retention](data-retention-and-deletion-policy.md), [request](data-subject-request-procedure.md) and [biometric governance](../governance/biometric-attendance-approval-evidence-checklist.md) prerequisites before use. Filling out this draft does not meet those prerequisites.
