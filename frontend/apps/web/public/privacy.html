Who we are

SyncCam AI ("SyncCam", "we") is provided by [Company legal name], [registered address]. For privacy questions or requests, contact [privacy contact email].

Our customers are businesses that use SyncCam with their own cameras. For the camera pictures and events at a customer's site, the customer decides how SyncCam is used and is responsible for telling people about it (for example with signs). We process that information on the customer's behalf. For our customers' account information, we are responsible ourselves.

What SyncCam is

SyncCam watches a customer's existing security cameras with AI that runs on a computer at the customer's site (the "site box"). It sends alerts, for example when someone enters a protected area at night, to the customer's dashboard and Telegram.

What stays at the customer's site

  • Video is not recorded by SyncCam. The AI reads the camera stream on the site box. Recording, if any, is done by the customer's own recorder.
  • Camera passwords are encrypted for the site box. Our servers only ever hold the encrypted form and cannot read them.
  • Live view sends pictures only while an authorised person is watching. Those pictures pass through our server's memory and are never stored. Every viewing session is logged.
  • Staff attendance (optional): if a customer turns it on, the face signatures of staff who have consented are stored only on the site box, encrypted. Only "this staff member was seen at this camera at this time" is sent to us. Faces of anyone else are ignored and not stored.

How AI decisions are used

SyncCam's AI can be wrong: it can miss events and raise false alerts. Alerts are suggestions for a person to review. SyncCam does not take actions against anyone on its own.

How long we keep it

We keep account and workspace information while the account is active. Alert records and alert pictures are currently kept until they are deleted; automatic deletion after a period the customer chooses is planned but not yet in place. A customer can ask us to delete them at any time. When a customer leaves, we delete their workspace data within [number] days, except records we must keep by law.

Security

Data sent between the site box, our servers and your browser is encrypted. Each workspace's data is separated in our database so customers cannot see each other's information. Administrator accounts must use an authenticator app. Changes to important settings are recorded in a tamper-evident log.
